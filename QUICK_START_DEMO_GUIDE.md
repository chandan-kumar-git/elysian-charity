## 📱 HOW TO USE

### **Step 1: Open the App**
The app is already running in Chrome. Just open the URL above or find the browser window.

### **Step 2: Navigate to Mobile Home**
1. On the landing page, click the **"Launch App"** button
2. You'll see the complete mobile phone interface!

### **Step 3: Explore All Features**
Click any of the 9 app icons:

| Icon | App | What You Can Do |
|------|-----|-----------------|
| 💰 | **Wallet** | View portfolio (\$12,458), send/receive crypto, swap tokens, check NFTs |
| 📱 | **Social** | Scroll feed, view stories, like posts (342, 891, 1245 likes) |
| 🛒 | **Market** | Browse NFTs, buy items (0.5-1.2 ETH), view collections |
| 💬 | **Messages** | Open chats, send messages, see online status |
| 📷 | **Camera** | Switch filters (Normal, Vivid, Cool, Warm, B&W) |
| 🗺️ | **Maps** | Find charity locations (2.3km, 4.1km, 1.8km away) |
| 🎵 | **Music** | Play/pause, skip songs, see playlist (3 songs) |
| 🎮 | **Games** | **Play trivia!** 3 questions, get trophy |
| ⚙️ | **Settings** | Toggle dark mode, notifications, biometrics |
